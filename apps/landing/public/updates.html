What's New

Latest updates and features.

Jul 20, 2026

The bot understands plain messages

No need to memorise commands. Type "balance" or "I want to return a voucher" and the bot replies with the answer, plus a button that takes you to the right screen in the Mini App.

Jun 17, 2026

Feedback is now a chat thread

Send feedback from the Mini App and read replies in a chat-style thread. Uploaders now get notified when one of their vouchers is reported as not working, with the screenshot and the last 4 barcode digits so you know exactly which voucher was flagged.

Jun 4, 2026

Refunds either way when you report a voucher

If you report a voucher as already used, your coins are safe. You get a replacement when one is available, or a full refund when it isn't. Declining a replacement now gets you a refund too.

May 28, 2026

More vouchers accepted

Vouchers without a valid-from date, like Dunnes spend-based vouchers, no longer get rejected at upload.

May 25, 2026

My Uploads screen

See every voucher you've uploaded and mark the ones you've used yourself, so nobody wastes coins claiming them.

May 23, 2026

The Mini App is here

Tap Menu in the bot to open the new account hub. Check your coin balance, browse available vouchers, review claims and transactions, read the FAQ or send feedback, all without leaving Telegram.

May 16, 2026

Fairer claims

If the voucher you claimed turns out to be already used, we can reverse the claim. Your coins come back and the voucher goes back in the pool.

Apr 25, 2026

Added support for Three+ vouchers

The bot now accepts Three+ vouchers (€5 off €25) from Three. You can upload and claim them just like Dunnes vouchers.
